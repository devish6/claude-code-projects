## 3. 🔴 WHAT THIS RETIRES

**"THE CREATIVE IS FINE. DISTRIBUTION HALVED." (2026-08-30) IS DEAD.** It rested
on V50: reach collapsed while per-viewer response hit the best number the account
had ever measured (skip 0.474, likes/view 14.71% vs a 3.63% median). That was a
real distribution signature *for that post*, and it was generalised to the
account. It should not have been — the window it was measured over was dominated
by quiet cuts, and the kinetic posts in the same window were reaching 3–4x more
people the whole time. ⛔ Do not restate it.

🪤 **AND THE HALF OF IT THAT SURVIVES.** Per-viewer engagement on the quiet cuts
is still 3–5x the account median (V55 17.9% likes/view, V56 13.0%, V50 11.8%
against ~3.6%). So it is NOT that IG viewers who watch it dislike it — the ones
who watch respond harder than for anything else the account makes. **Few are
served it.** Value-to-the-viewer and served-to-the-viewer are different claims
and only the second one is failing.

## 4. What actually distinguishes the formats on IG

The one metric IG ranks hardest on early is the first three seconds, and it is
the one the quiet format loses on and the kinetic format wins on — quiet skip ran
0.474 → 0.571 → 0.706 across V50/V55/V56 as the cuts stacked up on the same
opening frame. High watch time among survivors does not buy distribution back.

⇒ **The open question is no longer "is the format working" — it is "does the
opening frame earn IG's first three seconds".** V58 is the cleanest test yet: six
grounds this account has never owned, and after the 09-08 re-render it opens
**light, with dark type**, which no cut in either format has ever done. Every one
of the 30 quiet scenes shipped before it was cream on a dark ground.

⛔ **But judge V58 on TikTok, not on IG.** IG has now refused this format four
times out of four while distributing kinetic normally; an IG bar mostly measures
that refusal again. TikTok is where the format demonstrably competes.
