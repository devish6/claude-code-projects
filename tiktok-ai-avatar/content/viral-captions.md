# Numevix — Viral Set Captions (V01–V06)

Copy-paste ready. Each video has a **TikTok** caption (short — only ~1 line shows
before "more") and an **Instagram** caption (longer, since Reels captions get read).

**Rules baked into these:**
- The first 4–6 words carry the hook. Everything after the fold is bonus.
- Every caption ends in a question, because comments outrank likes for reach.
- **No links in TikTok captions** — they aren't clickable and outbound links can
  suppress distribution. Keep `numevix.com` in the bio only.
- Hashtags: 3–5, mixing one broad tag with niche ones. Thirty tags reads as spam.

## Posting notes

- **Add trending audio in-app.** These render with a licensed-free music bed only;
  trending sounds can't be baked into the file. Swap or layer in the TikTok editor.
- **Post the same video to both platforms**, but use the platform-specific caption —
  Reels captions get read, TikTok captions mostly get skipped.
- **Reply to early comments with a real number.** Someone commenting their birth date
  is a warm lead; answering with their actual Moolank is the single highest-converting
  thing you can do in the first hour, and it also multiplies the comment count.
- **Pin one comment** with "Full reading → numevix.com" so the link lives in the
  comment thread rather than the caption.
