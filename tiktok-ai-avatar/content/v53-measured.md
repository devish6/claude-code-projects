## 4. ⚖️ This overturns a rule on the books, deliberately

"A shorter cut holds more" was retired in an earlier era on Instagram VIEW
COUNTS — an instrument this account has since shown cannot separate creative
from distribution (σ log₁₀ ≈ 0.6; a 2× effect needs 62 posts per arm). The
evidence above is average-percentage-viewed and completion, conditioned on the
views actually served, agreeing across three platforms at once.

**Re-derive a rule's PREMISE, not its wording.** The premise that retired this
one is gone.

## 5. ⛔ The reach drop is NOT Instagram-specific

TikTok's 7-day views are down 44% over the same window Instagram's daily reach
halved. Whatever this is, it is not an Instagram throttle, and it is not
diagnosable from either platform alone. It remains a reason not to judge on
views — not a reason to stop shipping.

**TikTok is also 3× Instagram's distribution** (~2.7K/week vs ~850/week) and is
being treated as the secondary platform. Worth revisiting.
